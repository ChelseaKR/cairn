## Help meeting your basic needs

General Assistance provides temporary cash assistance for individuals who have little or no money. It can be approved for up to 90 days in a 12-month period, and possibly longer for people with disabilities.

## Who may be eligible for General Assistance

Residents of Sonoma County for at least 15 days; U.S. citizens or those approved as Lawful Permanent Residents; Adults age 18 or older; Married couples under age 18; Minors ineligible for other aid

We will review to see if: You meet the income and resource limits of the program, including: Reviewing any money received; Cash and bank balances of $50 or less; Personal property of $500 or less; One vehicle (or two vehicles) with total value of less than $3000. Any vehicle used as a home is not counted.

You may qualify for SonomaWORKS instead if: You are pregnant; Are caring for a child under 18

## Services you may receive can include:

A monthly cash grant for General Assistance, paid on an EBT card, to help pay for essentials such as food, personal items, or transportation; Payment to a landlord for rent; Payment towards utilities; CalFresh and Medi-Cal, if eligible; Assistance through Job Link to prepare for and find a job; Help with applying for Supplemental Security Income (SSI)

## How to apply

In Person: SonomaWORKS/Job Link office, 2227 Capricorn Way, Suite 100, Santa Rosa, CA 95407

Hours to apply: Monday – Friday, 8:00am – 5:00pm

Other locations to request an application
