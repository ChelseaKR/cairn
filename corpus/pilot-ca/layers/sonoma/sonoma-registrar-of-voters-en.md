## Our Office Has Moved!

Effective February 2, 2026, the Sonoma County Registrar of Voters is located at 3880 Brickway Blvd, Santa Rosa, CA 95403. View the press release announcing the move ( en español ).

November 3, 2026, General Election

Candidate nominations for most local offices closed on Friday, August 7. However, for certain offices, the nomination period was extended until Wednesday, August 12, because one or more eligible incumbents did not file. Read the press release ( en español ).

Offices that went to extension are indicated with a ***Nomination deadline extended to August 12*** label on the List of Local Candidates Who Have Filed page.
