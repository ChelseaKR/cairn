Covered California is the one-stop website where Californians can buy brand-name, private health insurance under the federal Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act. Anyone can buy health insurance through Covered California, but eligibility for financial help is based on income.

## Find out if you qualify

Visit CoveredCA.com to: • Learn about health plan options

• See if you qualify for a discount on private health insurance

• Find out if you're eligible for California's low-/no-cost Medi-Cal program

## Special Enrollment

Open enrollment starts in the fall, but you may be eligible for special enrollment if you experience a qualifying life event, such as: Marriage or divorce; Having a baby; Losing health care coverage; Moving to another county within California; Moving to California from out of state

## Health Insurance Options in Sonoma County

In Sonoma County, these companies offer health plans through Covered California: Anthem; Blue Shield of California; Health Net Life; Kaiser Permanente; Western Health Advantage

## For Small Businesses

Covered California also makes affordable health coverage available to small business employers and their employees.

Visit Covered California for Small Business for more information.

## To Apply for Covered California

To apply, fill out the online application on the Covered California website or call (800) 300-1506.

## To get in-person help to apply, visit:

Economic Assistance, 2550 Paulin Drive, Santa Rosa; Human Services South County Center, 5350 Old Redwood Highway, Suite 100, Petaluma; One of many community locations around Sonoma County to contact a no-cost Certified Enrollment Counselors.
