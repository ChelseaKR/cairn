The community health centers and clinics in Sonoma County are dedicated to providing high quality, affordable health services to families and children. They provide a wide range of services, but not all services are available at every location. Please contact your nearest health center to inquire about available services.

Those listed with an asterisk * offer limited immunization services to the public, but encourage establishing care as well.

The below information is not to be considered a complete list of locations where vaccinations are available. Most private health providers and many commercial pharmacies also offer vaccinations, and may be contacted to check for availability.

The Department of Health Services makes no assumption of quality and makes no warranties regarding providers appearing on this list. This list is simply a public listing provided for customer convenience, and is not a recommendation or endorsement by the Department.
