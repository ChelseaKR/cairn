## What is Medi-Cal?

Medi-Cal is Medicaid for California. It gives free or low-cost health coverage to people who qualify.

## What does Medi-Cal cover?

Medi-Cal pays for doctor visits, dental care, eye exams, medicine, and more.

## Can you get Medi-Cal ?

You may get Medi-Cal if you live in California and meet the rules.
