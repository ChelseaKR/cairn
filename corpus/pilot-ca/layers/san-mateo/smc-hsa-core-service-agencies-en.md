Core Service Agencies across the County of San Mateo can help you access food, housing assistance, crisis support, and other key resources.

We can help with: Food and groceries; Emergency housing and shelter; Utility bill assistance; Referrals to additional services

###### Video en español

Start here: Contact a Core Service Agency

If you are experiencing homelessness or are at risk of losing housing, your local agency can help. Find your area below and contact them to get started.
