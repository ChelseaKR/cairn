San Mateo County's Resource Centers provide employment-assistance services, and referrals to community-based agencies for assistance with additional needs, all of which are free of charge, and open to all members of the public.

#### What Services does the Resource Center Provide?

On-site employer hiring events; Help to find a career path; Resume Assistance; Job Search / Job Prep Workshops; Computers, Copiers, Fax Machines, & Phones; Guest Speakers and Workshops; Referrals to community-based agencies for assistance with other needs

#### Who Can Use the Resource Centers?

Open to all members of the public free of charge!

#### Are You an Employer?

Our staff can help host, coordinate and advertise an on-site hiring event at no cost to you!

Call one of the numbers listed above.
