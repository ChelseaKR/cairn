Specialists in employment services are waiting to help you.

Offering specialized training, job search workshops, computer/fax access and individual support

Finding and keeping a job, training for a job that is both rewarding and pays a livable wage, preparing yourself for today's changing economy… These are just a few of the ways we can help you.

Our partners at The Nova Job Center, a one-stop career transition workforce, work closely with local businesses, educators, and job seekers to ensure that our programs provide opportunities that build the knowledge, skills, and attitudes necessary to address the workforce needs of Silicon Valley. Check it out at novaworks.org.

To meet the needs of a changing economy and workforce, we offer new employment programs like SMC Works which provides subsidized employment to parents with children (under the age of 19).
