The Mission of the Siskiyou County Public Health Department is to promote and improve the health and wellness of the people of Siskiyou County through community empowerment and meaningful partnerships.

Our Vision is f or all Siskiyou County residents to live healthy, connected, and equitable lives.

At Siskiyou County Public Health, our work is guided by our Values of Excellence , Community , Service , Innovation , Collaboration, and Equity.
