Notice on Electioneering and Activity Relating to Corruption of the Voting Process

The Elections page provides information to Siskiyou County residents regarding the election process, voter registration and election results. Key document for the Current Election including important dates and candidate information as well as community elections are maintained on the Current Election page. All other pages and links are informational. The Secretary of State link provides additional election and voter information.

## Run for an Office - Getting Started

Interested in Running for Office? For local offices, take a look at the Candidate Guidebook that is posted with the most current election. For State Offices, refer to the information available on the CA Secretary of State’s website .
