The Assessor-Recorder Department is composed of two divisions. The functions of the divisions are diverse enough that they are treated to their own pages on the departmental web. Please utilize the Assessor-Recorder Navigation on the left sidebar to access specific areas of interest.

Announcement - Effective January 1, 2026, the Fees for Vital Records will increase by $2.00 each. Birth Certificates will be $31.00, Death Certificates will be $26.00, and Marriage Certificates will be $19.00.

Real Estate Protection Service - The Assessor-Recorder's office, through ParcelQuest, is excited to announce a new service that alerts you by email if a potentially fraudulent document is recorded which affects a property or name you have registered. The name of this service is Guard Dog and is available free of charge. Visit https://guarddog.parcelquest.com/ to enroll.

Value Notice - Annual Value Notices are now available online. Navigate to the Assessor Division: Lookup Parcels, Values and Maps Online section from the left hand menu.
