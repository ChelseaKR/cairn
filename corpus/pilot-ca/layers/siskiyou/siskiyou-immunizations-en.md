## Immunization Clinics

The Siskiyou County Public Health Immunization Program offers immunizations to community members at monthly immunization clinics in Yreka and Mt. Shasta. Vaccinations against polio, diphtheria, tetanus, measles, mumps, rubella, pertussis (whooping cough), pneumonia, hepatitis A&B, meningitis, human papilloma virus (HPV), chicken pox, shingles and flu are available. Travel vaccinations are also provided when available.

Children are encouraged to see their primary care providers for immunizations. Vaccines provided through the Vaccines for Children (VFC) Program and the Vaccines for Adults (VFA/317) Program are available for $9.00, or at no cost, to eligible children and adults. Children covered with private health insurance are not eligible to receive immunization services through these programs. Please contact your primary medical provider to obtain vaccinations. Some vaccines are available for retail cost, plus an administration fee, to individuals who are not eligible for the VFC or VFA/317 Programs.

Please call for eligibility guidelines, vaccine availability, and current fees.

Immunizations are by appointment only. Please call 530-841-2134 to schedule an appointment.

## California School Immunization Requirements

California law requires all children enrolled in state schools, both public and private, to have certain doctor-recommended immunizations, or receive them when they enroll.

Pre-Kindergarten (any private or public child care center, day nursery, nursery school, family day care home, or development center) requirements; K-12th Grade (including transitional Kindergarten) requirements

## Digital Vaccine Records

The California Department of Public Health (CDPH) has expanded the digital COVID vaccine record portal to include all vaccines, thereby providing digital access to one’s immunization record from the California Immunization Registry (CAIR). This new Digital Vaccine Record (DVR) portal provides a much-desired service, giving California residents easy access to their immunization history.
