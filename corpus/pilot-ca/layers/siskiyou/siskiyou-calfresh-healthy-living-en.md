***Dear Cal Fresh Healthy Living community partners: Due to recent federal funding changes, the Cal Fresh Healthy Living program is ending and will receive no new funding for program activities after October 1, 2025. This means that we will not be doing any direct or indirect education (including Harvest of the Month) after that point.

Program wrap-up will take place between October 1, 2025, and April 30, 2026. During this period, we would like to support your ongoing efforts to promote healthy eating, activity, and environments in our communities. Within funding limitations, we can help by purchasing items for school and/or community gardens as well as approved curriculums and supplies related to nutrition and physical education. We also invite you to participate in conversations about your experiences and anticipated needs so that your voices can be included as we conduct final reporting.

Our community is fortunate to have each of you, and we are grateful that we had the opportunity to partner with you in this work***

CalFresh Healthy Living (SNAP-Ed) supports healthy, active, and nourished lifestyles by teaching Californians about good nutrition and how to stretch their food dollars, while also building partnerships in communities to make the healthy choice, the easy choice.

Learn more about CalFresh Healthy Living >>

​ CalFresh Healthy Living Campaigns & Programs include: Harvest of the Month (HOTM); Rethink Your Drink (RYD); School Garden Support; School Wellness Support; Physical Activity & Nutrition Curriculum; Playground Stencils

CalFresh Healthy Living participates in and supports local events such as: Field Days; Health Fairs; Farmer's Market; Bike to School Day; Walk to School Day; Summer Programs

Through statewide, regional, and local partnerships, programs, and policy initiatives, we promote healthy eating, physical activity, and food security with an emphasis on communities with the greatest health disparities.
