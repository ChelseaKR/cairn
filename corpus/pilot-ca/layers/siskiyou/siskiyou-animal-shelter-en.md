Animals on this page are looking for their families. If you believe one of these animals may be yours, please contact our animal shelter at 530-841-4028 . We hold all stray animals for a minimum of five (5) business days, and horses and certain livestock for fourteen (14) business days. Proof of ownership is mandatory. Proof of a current rabies vaccination is required at the time of reclaim for all dogs four (4) months and older.
