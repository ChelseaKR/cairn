The Fresh Start Grocery Allowance helps Harbor County households buy food. It
is a monthly benefit loaded onto a payment card you can use at participating
grocery stores and farmers markets.

## How much you get

A one-person household receives $212 per month. Each additional household
member adds $118 per month. For example, a household of three receives $448
per month.

## Who can apply

Your household's gross monthly income must be at or below $2,430 for one
person, plus $860 for each additional member. You must live in Harbor County.
You do not need a permanent address to apply.

## How to apply and what happens next

Apply online, by mail, or in person at any Community Assistance office. The
county makes a decision within 30 days of receiving a complete application.
If approved, benefits start the first day of the following month, and you
must renew your enrollment every 12 months.
