The Harbor Housing Relief Grant is a one-time payment that helps renters who
have fallen behind on rent stay in their homes. The grant is paid directly to
your landlord.

## How much the grant covers

The grant covers up to $3,500 of unpaid rent. It is paid once per household;
a household that has received the grant before cannot receive it again for
36 months.

## Who can apply

You must rent your home in Harbor County under a current written lease, be at
least one full month behind on rent, and your household income must be at or
below 60 percent of the county median income.

## Deadlines

Applications are accepted from March 1 through September 30 each year, or
until the year's funds run out, whichever comes first. A complete application
is processed within 21 days.
