The Harbor Winter Utility Credit lowers the cost of heating a home during the
coldest months. The credit is applied straight to your utility account by the
power company, so there is no cheque to cash and no money passes through you.

## What the credit is worth

An eligible household receives a credit of $95 per month from November through
March, for a maximum of $475 in a single winter. Households that heat with
electricity receive an extra $40 per month.

## Who qualifies

You qualify if you pay a residential utility bill in Harbor County and your
household income is at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty level.
Renters whose heating is included in their rent qualify through their landlord
instead, and should ask the landlord to apply.

## Applying, and what a decision takes

Applications open on October 1 and close on February 28. Apply through your
utility company or at any Community Assistance office. A decision takes 14
days, and the credit appears on the next full billing cycle after approval.
