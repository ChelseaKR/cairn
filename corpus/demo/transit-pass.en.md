The Harbor GoPass gives eligible riders a reduced fare on all Harbor County
buses and the harbor ferry.

## The discount and the fee

GoPass holders pay 50 percent of the standard fare on every ride. The pass
costs $20 per year, and the fee is waived for riders enrolled in the Fresh
Start Grocery Allowance.

## Who is eligible

Riders age 65 or older, riders with a qualifying disability, and members of
any household currently enrolled in a Community Assistance benefit program
are eligible for the GoPass.

## Where to get one

Apply at the transit center on Dock Street or at any Community Assistance
office. Bring photo identification. Passes are issued the same day, and a
replacement for a lost pass costs $5.
